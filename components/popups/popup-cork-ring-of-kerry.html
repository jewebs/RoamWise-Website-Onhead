Service description

Ring of Kerry Private Tour

Discover the breathtaking beauty of Ireland's southwest coast on an unforgettable 8-hour private tour through the famous Ring of Kerry.

Travel comfortably with an experienced local guide as you explore spectacular coastal landscapes, historic castles, charming villages, and hidden gems away from the usual tourist routes.

What to Expect

Discover Historic Landmarks

Explore Cahergall Fort, an impressive ancient stone fort, and enjoy a photo stop at the picturesque ruins of Ballycarbery Castle near Cahersiveen.

Explore Coastal Villages & Islands

Visit the charming fishing village of Portmagee and discover Valentia Island, known for its dramatic cliffs, stunning ocean views, and fascinating history.

Experience the Skellig Ring

Travel along one of Ireland's most scenic coastal routes, with breathtaking views of the Atlantic Ocean and opportunities to discover beautiful places such as Ballinskelligs.

Local Flavours & Natural Beauty

Enjoy a stop at Skelligs Chocolate before continuing towards the magnificent Killarney National Park, famous for its lakes, mountains, and beautiful landscapes.

A Personalized Journey

Travel beyond the main tourist routes with a knowledgeable local guide who will share fascinating stories, local traditions, and insights into the region's heritage.
