Service description

Discover the breathtaking beauty of Northern Ireland on a private 8-hour tour along the famous Causeway Coastal Route.

Explore dramatic coastal landscapes, historic castles, charming fishing villages, and the UNESCO World Heritage Site of Giant's Causeway, all with the comfort and flexibility of a private tour.

What to Expect

Scenic Coastal Journey

Travel along the stunning Causeway Coastal Route, enjoying spectacular ocean views, picturesque villages, and the natural beauty of the Glens of Antrim.

Discover Giant's Causeway

Visit the famous Giant's Causeway, a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its remarkable hexagonal basalt columns, formed by volcanic activity around 60 million years ago. Access to the natural site is free.

Carrick-a-Rede Rope Bridge

Experience the spectacular coastal scenery at Carrick-a-Rede, where you can cross the famous rope bridge for unforgettable views. Bridge tickets are available separately.

Historic Castles & Local Experiences

Discover landmarks such as Dunluce Castle and Carrickfergus Castle, explore charming coastal villages, and enjoy a traditional Irish pub lunch along the way.

Bushmills & Game of Thrones Locations

Depending on your interests and available time, your tour may include a stop at Bushmills Distillery or famous Game of Thrones filming locations.
