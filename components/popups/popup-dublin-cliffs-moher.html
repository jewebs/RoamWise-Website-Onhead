7:00am — Pickup Dublin

9:00am — Barack Obama Plaza (30 min duration)

Petrol station, built in tribute to the ex president of USA in the village of Moneygall as Barack's great-grandfather lived in and emigrated to the US in 1850. You will find a charge free museum.

11:30am — Cliffs of Moher (1:30 hour duration)

The Cliffs of Moher were formed over 300 million years ago from layers of sandstone, siltstone, and shale deposited by an ancient river. Over time, these sediments were compressed into rock and later uplifted. The Atlantic Ocean’s waves then eroded the cliffs, shaping their dramatic vertical faces seen today. It's higher point reaches 214 metres above the sea.

15:45 — Galway City centre (1:15 hour duration)

On the way from the Cliffs of Moher to Galway City we will pass through The Atlantic Way, a stunning coastal drive, passing through the Burren’s unique landscape and charming villages. It ends in Galway’s vibrant, culture-filled streets.

In Galway you will be free to walk through the pubs, restaurants and its charming pedestrian streets.

19:00 — Dublin City
