Service description

Cobh Titanic & Maritime History Walking Tour

Discover the fascinating maritime history of Cobh, a charming waterfront town in County Cork, on a guided walking tour exploring its connections to the Titanic, Irish emigration, and centuries of seafaring heritage.

Walk through historic streets, visit iconic landmarks, and uncover the stories that shaped this important Irish port.

What to Expect

Discover Cobh's Titanic History

Explore the original streets, buildings, and waterfront locations connected to the Titanic, including the historic pier where passengers boarded tenders to join the ship on its final voyage.

Explore Maritime Heritage

Learn about Cobh's connections to the Titanic and Lusitania, as well as the millions of Irish emigrants who departed from its harbour in search of new opportunities.

Historic Landmarks & Local Stories

Admire the impressive St. Colman's Cathedral, visit the Titanic Monument, and discover the history of Spike Island, military activity, and convict transportation.

A Guided Local Experience

Explore Cobh with insights from a walking tour developed by a local historian, bringing the town's remarkable past to life.

Tour Highlights

  • Guided walking tour through historic Cobh
  • Discover original Titanic-related locations
  • Visit the historic Titanic passenger departure pier
  • Admire St. Colman's Cathedral and the Titanic Monument
  • Learn about Irish emigration and maritime history
  • Discover the stories of Spike Island and convict transportation
  • Tour conducted in English

Experience Cobh's unique heritage through the stories, landmarks, and historic streets that connect this beautiful harbour town to Ireland's maritime past.
