Service description

Galway History & Legends Walking Tour

Discover the fascinating history, captivating legends, and hidden stories of Galway on a guided walking tour through its charming streets.

Enjoy a relaxed 75–90-minute experience as you explore the city's historic landmarks and uncover the stories that have shaped its unique character.

What to Expect

The Story of the Lynch Family

Discover one of Galway's most dramatic historical legends, involving love, jealousy, and a mysterious murder accusation. Follow the story of Walter Lynch and uncover the events behind this fascinating local tale.

The Legend of the Claddagh Ring

Learn about the origins of Ireland's famous Claddagh ring, a symbol of love, loyalty, and friendship, through stories of pirates, captivity, and an enduring romance.

Galway's Historic Connections

Explore the city's maritime heritage and discover its legendary connections to Christopher Columbus and his voyages of exploration.

A Relaxed Walking Experience

Stroll through Galway's historic streets at a comfortable pace, visiting important landmarks while enjoying engaging stories about the city's past.

Tour Highlights

  • Guided walking tour lasting 75–90 minutes
  • Discover the dramatic legend of the Lynch family
  • Learn the fascinating story behind the Claddagh ring
  • Explore Galway's connections to Christopher Columbus
  • Visit historic landmarks and charming streets
