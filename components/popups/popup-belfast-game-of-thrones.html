Service description

Step into the world of Game of Thrones on an unforgettable 8-hour private tour through Northern Ireland's most iconic filming locations. Travel comfortably in a private minivan with a knowledgeable local guide as you explore breathtaking landscapes, historic castles, and famous locations from the series.

What to Expect

Discover Iconic Filming Locations

Follow in the footsteps of your favorite characters as you visit the famous Dark Hedges (King's Road), Ballintoy Harbour (Iron Islands), and the fascinating Cushendun Caves.

Explore Giant's Causeway

Discover Northern Ireland's UNESCO World Heritage Site, famous for its approximately 40,000 interlocking basalt columns and spectacular coastal scenery.

Castles & Coastal Landscapes

Enjoy scenic stops at Dunluce Castle, Carrickfergus Castle, and Carrick-a-Rede Rope Bridge while exploring the beautiful Antrim Coast.

A Personalized Experience

Enjoy a flexible itinerary with opportunities for photos, local stories, and a traditional Irish pub lunch along the way.
